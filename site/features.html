Features

Capture. Mark up. Find it again.

Snapwright covers the whole life of a screenshot, from the keypress to the moment you need it again three weeks later.

Capture

Fast enough that you stop thinking about it.

Global hotkey

Ctrl Shift 2 on Windows and Linux, ⌘ Shift 2 on macOS. Works from any app.

Region select

Drag to select with a live size label. Release or press Enter to copy. Esc cancels.

Snap to window

Hover highlights the window under the pointer; one click captures exactly that window.

Full display

Click anywhere on the bare desktop to take the whole display.

Keyboard precision

Arrow keys nudge the selection a pixel at a time, Shift and arrows resize it. Hold Shift on release to keep adjusting.

Colour loupe

A magnifier follows your pointer with the exact pixel colour. Press C to copy it as hex.

Repeat last region

Ctrl Alt Shift 2 grabs the same region again with no overlay, straight to the clipboard.

Delayed capture

Capture in 3 seconds from the tray, with an on-screen countdown that is never in the shot.

Clipboard and file

Every capture is copied as PNG and saved to Pictures/Snapwright at the same time.

A loupe for the details

Matching a brand colour or checking alignment? The loupe magnifies the pixels around your pointer and shows their exact colour, so you never need a separate picker.

Countdown for the tricky shots

Menus, tooltips and hover states disappear the moment you press a key. Start a delayed capture from the tray, set up the screen, and Snapwright takes it for you.

Preview

Your capture, right where you need it.

Quick-access preview

After each capture, a small preview appears in the corner. Click it to open the capture in the library, or hit Edit to mark it up right away. It steps aside on its own, and it is never in your next shot.

  • Stays while you hover
  • One click to the editor
  • Closes before the next capture

Library

A home for every capture, searchable from day one.

Full-text search

Search app names, window titles, tags and notes, powered by SQLite FTS5.

Rich metadata

Each capture records the app and window it came from, the display, size and time.

Tags and notes

Organize captures your way and leave yourself context for later.

Trash and restore

Delete without worry; restore anything until you empty the trash.

Fast thumbnails

A virtual-scrolling grid stays smooth with thousands of captures.

Local-first

Your library lives on your machine, in your platform's data folder. No account needed.

Non-destructive

Edits are saved as a scene beside the original. Reopen and change them any time, or revert in one click.

Redaction built in

Blur and pixelate hide sensitive details before a capture leaves your screen.

Seven colours, three widths

A tight palette that looks good on any background, with crisp anti-aliased rendering.

Under the hood

Speed is the product, so we measure it.

Written in Rust

A native GPU-rendered interface with no browser engine inside. Small, fast and light on memory.

Kept warm

Capture devices, buffers and the GPU are set up once and reused, so every capture is as fast as the last.

One release pipeline

Builds for macOS, Windows and Linux come from one automated pipeline, with code signing and notarization built in.
